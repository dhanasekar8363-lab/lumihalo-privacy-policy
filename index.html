1. Introduction

LumiHalo is an ambient screen lamp application offering customizable lighting, colours, brightness controls and ambient visual effects. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

Local preferences: The app may store selected colours, brightness, animation preferences, favourites, reminder settings, haptic preferences and purchase-related status on your device.

Advertising: Free users may see banner advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect IP addresses, general location, advertising and device identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostics.

Purchases: Google Play Billing processes the one-time Premium purchase. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

3. How We Use Information

  • Save application preferences.
  • Provide lamp and customization features.
  • Manage Premium purchase access.
  • Display advertisements to eligible free users.
  • Maintain application performance and security.
  • Schedule reminders enabled by you.

4. Advertising and Consent

LumiHalo uses Google AdMob for advertising. Advertising personalization and consent choices depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements. Where required, a compliant consent management solution will be used before relevant advertising is served.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob – advertising.
  • Google Play Billing – in-app purchases.
  • Google Play Services – Android application functionality.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party services may process information to provide advertising, purchases and application functionality.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Application preferences are generally stored locally. Google and other service providers may retain information according to their own policies. Uninstalling the app may remove local preferences but does not necessarily delete information held by third parties.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information held by a service provider. You can manage available advertising preferences through Google and your device settings.

10. Children's Privacy

LumiHalo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you have concerns about information associated with a child.

11. International Data Transfers

Third-party services may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Their privacy policies explain their practices.
